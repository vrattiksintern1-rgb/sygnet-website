About Sygnet IoT. A full stack IoT platform company.

  • Who we are

    Sygnet IoT Pvt. Ltd. is an IoT platform company building a complete ecosystem of intelligent hardware, cloud software, mobile applications and operational intelligence solutions.

  • Sygnet IoT Connect

    Through its flagship platform, Sygnet IoT Connect, organizations can digitize assets, monitor operations in real time, automate workflows and gain actionable business insights.
