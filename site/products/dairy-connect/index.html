Model 1 · Kiosk

Community Milk ATM

Fixed installation for 24x7 community access

A chilled, self-service milk kiosk placed where people live. Customers bring their own container, pay without cash and take the quantity they want, at any hour, with no one needed at the counter.

Key benefits

Model 2 · On wheels

Mobile Milk ATM

On-wheels milk distribution, anywhere, anytime

A stainless milk tank and dispensing units on a light vehicle. It takes chilled milk along a route, to a colony or to an event, and customers fill and pay at the vehicle.

Key benefits

  • Reach more customers
  • Flexible timings and routes
  • Fresh milk with chilled storage
  • Operates with minimal staff
  • Expand business faster

Ideal for

  • Doorstep delivery
  • Urban routes and colonies
  • Events and fairs
  • Rural outreach programs
  • Areas without permanent outlets

Locations

Urban routes, doorstep delivery, events, rural outreach

Sygnet Dairy Connect gives dairies and milk cooperatives automated Milk ATMs on the Sygnet IoT Connect platform: the Community Milk ATM, a chilled self-service kiosk for villages, housing societies and apartment complexes, and the Mobile Milk ATM, a milk tank with dispensing units on a vehicle for routes, doorstep delivery and events. Customers pay by UPI, QR code, wallet or RFID card and bring their own container. Each machine reports milk temperature, tank level, sales and alerts to one dashboard. Sygnet IoT is based in Surat, Gujarat, India.
