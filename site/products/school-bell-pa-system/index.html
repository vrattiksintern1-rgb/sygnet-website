TBG

Basic bell timer

Reliable bell automation for schools and industries

The TBG automatic bell timer rings the bell on your timetable, so no one has to watch the clock. You program it on its keypad, and its relay output drives the bell, hooter or siren you already have.

Features

  • Automatic bell scheduling
  • Multiple daily timetables
  • Relay output for bell/hooter/siren
  • RTC with battery backup
  • Manual bell trigger
  • Easy keypad programming
  • Compact wall-mount design

Applications

  • Schools
  • Colleges
  • Factories
  • Workshops

Best for

Customers who only need automatic bell ringing.

TBA-WL

Audio bell timer

Bell automation with MP3 audio playback

The TBA-WL audio bell timer adds recorded audio to the bell. Prayers, the national anthem and recorded announcements play from MP3 at the set time, through its speaker output or your amplifier.

Features

  • MP3 audio playback
  • Prayer and national anthem scheduling
  • 32 GB SD card storage
  • Mobile app audio upload
  • Built-in speaker output
  • AUX output for amplifier
  • LCD display and keypad
  • Fully automatic operation

Best for

Schools wanting automated prayers and announcements without a full PA system.

TBA-D

Desktop bell & PA system

Classroom communication made easy

The TBA-D desktop bell and PA system does everything the TBA-WL does and adds a desktop microphone. Staff speak live to one classroom, a group of rooms or a zone, through addressable speakers.

Features

  • All TBA-WL features
  • Desktop microphone
  • Live announcements
  • Addressable speaker support
  • Classroom-wise communication
  • Zone and group announcements
  • Local audio monitoring
  • Volume control

Best for

Medium and large schools requiring classroom-wise announcements.

TBW-WL

Smart Wi-Fi bell system

Mobile app controlled bell and announcement system

The TBW-WL smart Wi-Fi bell system is run from a mobile app: timetables, audio uploads and live announcements, over Wi-Fi or from anywhere with internet access. It keeps its own time in sync.

Features

  • Mobile app control
  • Unlimited timetables
  • Wireless audio upload
  • Live mobile announcements
  • External mic support
  • Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Remote internet access
  • Automatic time synchronisation

Touch screen bell & PA system

The ultimate smart school communication platform

The TBW-D premium touch screen bell and PA system is the top of the range. It is run from a 5-inch touch screen with a graphical timetable editor, or from the mobile app, and drives addressable speakers for zone, group and live announcements.

Features

  • Touch + mobile app operation
  • Graphical timetable editor

Sygnet School Bell & PA systems are automatic bell timers and public address systems for schools, colleges, universities, factories and workshops. The range runs from the TBG automatic bell timer, through the TBA-WL MP3 audio bell timer and the TBA-D desktop bell and PA system, to the TBW-WL smart Wi-Fi bell system and the TBW-D touch screen bell and PA system, with addressable classroom speakers and a mobile app. Sygnet IoT is based in Surat, Gujarat, India.
