Sygnet Textile Connect is an industrial IoT platform for textile mills and factories. It brings production, energy, utility and business intelligence together: machine, production and OEE monitoring, energy and power quality, compressors, DG sets, solar, water and RO plants, with ERP integration, executive dashboards, alerts and reports on the web and on Android and iOS. It covers spinning, warping and sizing, weaving, dyeing and processing, knitting, embroidery and garment units. Sygnet IoT is based in Surat, Gujarat, India.
