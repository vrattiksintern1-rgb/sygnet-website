How a call works. From the bed to the record.

  1. 01The patient callsThe patient presses the bed switch or pulls the bathroom cord.
  2. 02The ward sees itThe room indicator lights up and the nurse station display shows the bed and ward.
  3. 03A nurse attendsThe nurse goes to the bed and presses the attend switch (Enterprise).
  4. 04The call is recordedThe call is kept in the reporting server.

Benefits on every ward. What the hospital gets from Sygnet nurse call.

  • Nursing staff performance monitoringEvery call is recorded, so nursing managers can see how calls are answered.
  • Smoother patient experiencePatients know their call has gone through and that help is coming.
  • Turnaround and waiting-time optimisationCalls are seen at once, which helps the ward shorten the wait between a call and care.
  • Reporting software on a local serverCall records stay inside the hospital, on a server on site.
  • Staff app for on-the-move alerts EnterpriseCode Blue calls reach staff on their phones, away from the desk.

Sygnet NCS is an IoT-enabled nurse call system for hospital wards, wired or wireless, in three tiers. NCS-Plus pairs a single-service nurse station display for 8, 16 or 24 rooms with room indicators and hand-held pendant bed switches. NCS-Professional adds an LCD multi-service nursing display, bed switches for nurse, housekeeping and Code Blue calls, bedside pendants, bathroom pull-cords and a standard reporting server. NCS-Enterprise adds Code Blue switches, a nurse station display unit, patient and staff trackers, attend switches, a staff app for Code Blue and an enterprise reporting server. Sygnet IoT is based in Surat, Gujarat, India.
