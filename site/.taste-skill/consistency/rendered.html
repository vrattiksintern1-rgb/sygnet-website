How the platform works

Connect. Monitor. Automate. Scale.

Sygnet IoT Connect links devices, sensors and machines to one secure platform, runs automation at the edge and turns the data into apps your teams use every day.

One platform under every product. Five layers, from the device to AI.

  1. DeviceConnect any device, sensor, machine or industrial system.
  2. EdgeRun automation and process data locally, so sites keep working when the link drops.
  3. CloudStore, manage and process device data on a secure platform.
  4. ApplicationsIndustry suites and custom apps built on the same data.
  5. AIPredict, optimise and automate from what the devices report.
