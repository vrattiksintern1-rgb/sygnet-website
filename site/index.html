Connect. Monitor. Automate. Scale.

From Sensors to Insights. From Data to Decisions. Sygnet IoT Connect links devices, sensors and machines to one secure platform and turns their data into apps your teams use every day.

The platform

One platform under every product. Five layers, from the device to AI.

  1. 01DeviceConnect any device, sensor, machine or industrial system.
  2. 02EdgeRun automation and process data locally, so sites keep working when the link drops.
  3. 03CloudStore, manage and process device data on a secure platform.
  4. 04ApplicationsIndustry suites and custom apps built on the same data.
  5. 05AIPredict, optimise and automate from what the devices report.
